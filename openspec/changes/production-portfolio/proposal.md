# Production portfolio contracts and accounting

## Why
The baseline exposes only trade recording and a summary, with average-cost accounting and zero market valuation. Publish the currently callable HTTP contract and evolve the service through explicit domain, cash, valuation and operational requirements without claiming unimplemented routes work.

## Scope
Establish the versioned API contract first, then implement instruments, lot accounting, corporate actions, deterministic valuation/returns, payment-backed cash movement and operational hardening. All money uses exact decimals and explicit currencies. S3 consumption depends on payment contract publication and integration verification.

## Out of scope
Real broker, price-feed, payment or ledger connectivity; real money or personal data; claims of durable recovery from memory-only storage.
